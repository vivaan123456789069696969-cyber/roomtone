# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our commitment

We want Roomtone to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their background, identity, experience or
ability.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assume good intent, and focus on what is best for users and the community.
- Respect privacy: never share anyone's photos, home details or personal information.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainers. All reports are reviewed promptly and confidentially. Maintainers may
remove contributions or ban participants who break this code.
